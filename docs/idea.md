What to capture for every idea
The problem:

Problem, in the owner's own words
Evidence: where you heard it and how many times (1 person or 10?)
Frequency: daily, weekly, monthly or yearly
What they do today: Excel, paper, a staff member, an existing tool
Current spend: how much they already pay to deal with it (₹/month). This is the strongest sign they'll pay.
The solution:
6. Idea, in one line
7. Difficulty to build, out of 5, for you working solo
8. Time to first version: weeks
9. Competition: none, weak or strong (name the competitors)
10. Price they'd pay, ₹/month

The risk:
11. Switching pain: how hard it is for them to move away from what they use now
12. Must-have or nice-to-have: does it make them money or save them money, or is it just convenient?
