# Call guide: developers whose MahaRERA registration is about to lapse

For the first 10 conversations. The base of every call is a public, checkable fact about **their** project: its completion date on MahaRERA, plus the automatic 4-month extension (Order 66/2026), gives a date by which they must extend or upload the occupancy certificate. We bring that fact, explain their options, and in doing so learn how they work. See [README.md](README.md) for the problem and [workflow-research.md](workflow-research.md) for the flows.

## Before the call (2 minutes per lead)

From the lead sheet: project name and MahaRERA number, completion date, **lapse date** (completion + 4 months if the date is on/after 28 Feb 2026), whether an extension certificate exists, promoter name, and the person to ask for. Open the project on MahaRERA's search to be sure the details still match.

## The call

**1. Opening (why you're calling: their date, not your product)**
> "Hello, I'm Prakash. I'm calling about **[Project]**, MahaRERA number **[P5…]**. As per MahaRERA's public record its completion date was **[31 Jul 2026]**; with the automatic 4-month extension that runs to **[30 Nov 2026]**, and I don't see an extension filed. Who looks after your MahaRERA filings? I'd like to make sure they're aware."

If they say "our CA handles it": "Great. Could I speak to you or the CA for two minutes? After that date the project goes into abeyance: sales registrations and the RERA account are frozen."

**2. The three diagnosis questions** (you need these to tell them the right route, so they're natural to ask):
1. "Is construction complete, and have you received the occupancy certificate?" → if yes: the fix is uploading the OC + Form 4, not an extension.
2. "Roughly how many flats are sold, and are most buyers on home loans?" → decides the extension route (Section 6, or 7(3) with 51% buyer consent) and tells us about their collections.
3. "Who prepares your quarterly reports and MahaRERA filings: your CA, a consultant, or someone in-house?"

**3. Give value immediately** (this is what earns the rest of the call):
- Finished + OC: "Then it's paperwork: upload the OC and Form 4 before [date], file any missing QPRs, and the project closes cleanly."
- Not finished, first delay: "Section 6 allows up to a year with documented reasons; no buyer consent needed. It has to be filed before [date]."
- Not finished, already extended: "Section 7(3): ideally 51% of buyers' consent, though MahaRERA can allow it without. It needs a delay explanation, a revised schedule and disclosure of complaints."
- "Note: an extension doesn't change the possession date in buyers' agreements. MahaRERA has ordered interest even on extended projects."

**4. The workflow questions** (ask 2–3, only once they're talking):
- "When a slab is done, how do demand letters go out to buyers? Who makes them, and how long does a round take?"
- "For buyers on home loans, how long do bank disbursements take per stage? What usually holds them up?"
- "How do you keep track of who has paid and who's overdue? Do you charge late interest?"
- "What does your CA or consultant charge for the quarterly reports? Has a withdrawal from the RERA account ever got stuck?"
- "What software do you use today: Tally, Excel, anything for post-sales?"

**5. Close (the free diagnosis)**
> "I'll send you a one-page note on exactly what [Project] needs before [date] and the documents to keep ready. No charge. What's the best email or WhatsApp?"

Write their answers in the sheet's notes column during the call.

## The one-page diagnosis (sent after the call)

**[Project] (MahaRERA [P5…]) · prepared [date] for [Name]**

1. **Status on MahaRERA:** completion date [date]; automatic extension to [lapse date] (Order 66/2026); extension certificate: [none / yes].
2. **What happens after [lapse date]:** registration in abeyance; sub-registrar stops registering sales; RERA account frozen; marketing barred; buyers can still claim delay interest.
3. **Your route out:** [OC + Form 4 upload / Section 6 extension / Section 7(3) extension], based on what you told us.
4. **Documents to keep ready:** [route-specific list: OC and architect's Form 4; or delay explanation, revised schedule (PERT), buyer consents in Format B, list of complaints/litigation, notarised declaration, up-to-date QPRs and Form 5].
5. **Deadline plan:** what must be filed by when, working back from [lapse date].
6. **Next step:** "We can prepare and file this for you, coordinating your architect, engineer and CA. Reply to this note or call [your phone]."

## Rules for every call

- Only state what the public record shows; say "as per MahaRERA's public record" and let them correct you (they may have filed something not yet reflected).
- Never claim to be from MahaRERA or a government body.
- Ask for the person who handles filings; never pitch the sales desk.
- WhatsApp only after they've agreed to it.
