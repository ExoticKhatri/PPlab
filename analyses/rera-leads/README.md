# RERA outreach: the problem we solve and what we pitch

Briefing written 9 Nov 2026 before first contact with developers. Sources are linked; anything marked **unconfirmed** must be checked against MahaRERA's own orders before we promise it to anyone. Lead data lives in `raw/` (gitignored).

## 1. Who we are talking to

Developers (builders) in Maharashtra whose project MahaRERA has put on one of its public "problem" lists ([MahaRERA](https://www.maharera.maharashtra.gov.in), lists updated 4 Nov 2026):

| List | Projects | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Abeyance: completion date lapsed | 4,237 | The completion date the developer promised MahaRERA has passed, and they neither finished (OC uploaded) nor got an extension |
| Abeyance: quarterly reports (QPR) not filed | 1,632 | Suspended for not filing the mandatory quarterly progress reports |
| Recent QPR defaulters | 212 | Newly non-compliant |

In May 2026 MahaRERA also issued show-cause notices to **8,212 projects** for missing the Jan–Mar 2026 QPR (due 20 April) ([Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/invest/maharera-flags-8212-projects-for-missing-qpr-deadline-gives-regulatory-warning)).

## 2. What their problem actually is

Every RERA project is registered with a promised completion date and must report progress every quarter. When a developer misses either, MahaRERA puts the project **in abeyance** (registration frozen):

- **Sales stop:** MahaRERA directs the sub-registrar not to register sale deeds or agreements in the project, so no new flat can be sold and no buyer can register their purchase ([91acres](https://91acres.com/news/maharera-suspends-4812-lapsed-projects-freezes-bank-accounts-20350); [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/invest/maharera-flags-8212-projects-for-missing-qpr-deadline-gives-regulatory-warning)).
- **Money stops:** the project's RERA bank account (where 70% of buyer payments sit) is **frozen**, so the developer can't withdraw construction money ([91acres](https://91acres.com/news/maharera-suspends-4812-lapsed-projects-freezes-bank-accounts-20350)).
- **Marketing stops:** advertising and marketing of the project are barred.
- **Penalty:** ₹50,000 for the QPR default ([Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/invest/maharera-flags-8212-projects-for-missing-qpr-deadline-gives-regulatory-warning)).
- **Escalation:** if nothing happens, registration can be **revoked** and MahaRERA can even appoint someone else to complete the project ([Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/mumbai/maharera-keeps-registration-of-1750-lapsed-housing-projects-in-abeyance)).
- **Buyers still get interest:** an extension of registration does **not** move the possession date in buyers' agreements. MahaRERA (July 2026, Mont Vert Belcreek, Pune) ordered interest to 26 buyers for delay even though the project had an extension ([K&S Partners](https://ksandk.com/newsletter/maharera-registration-extension-possession-date/); [LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/amp/consumer-cases/homebuyers-can-claim-interest-for-delayed-possession-despite-project-deadline-extension-maharera-516517)).

In plain words: **a project in abeyance can't sell, can't touch its money and can't advertise.** For a developer with unsold flats, every week costs real money. For one whose project is actually finished, it's a paperwork problem that still blocks resales and buyer registrations.

## 3. Why they fall into it (our hypotheses, to confirm on calls)

1. **The project is done, but the paperwork isn't.** They got the occupancy certificate (OC) but never uploaded it with the architect's completion certificate (Form 4), so MahaRERA still thinks the project is open. *(Hypothesis: likely common among small developers.)*
2. **Construction is late,** and they didn't apply for an extension before the date passed.
3. **Small builders have no compliance person.** QPRs need Forms 1 (architect), 2 (engineer) and 3 (CA) every quarter, and the deadline is days after the quarter ends.
4. **The portal changed.** MahaRERA moved everything to a new portal, **MahaCRITI**; the old portal stopped accepting anything on 10 May 2026, and pending old applications were closed (Order 65A/2026) ([IndiaLaw](https://www.indialaw.in/blog/real-estate/maharera-order-65a2026/)). Some developers simply lost track.
5. **The project is genuinely stuck** (money, land or legal problems). These are hard and not our first customers.

## 4. How a project gets out (the work we would do)

| Situation | Way out | What's involved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Project finished | **Close it properly** | Upload the OC and Form 4 (architect's completion certificate), bring QPRs up to date, request the status update. *Unconfirmed: exact closure steps on MahaCRITI.* |
| Late, first delay | **Extension under Section 6** (force majeure, max 1 year) | Application on MahaCRITI with documented reasons; no buyer consent needed ([MahaRERA](https://maharera.maharashtra.gov.in/project-extension)) |
| Late again | **Extension under Section 7(3)** | Ideally consent of 51% of buyers (Format B, Circular 28/2021); since Orders 40/2022 and 40A/2023 MahaRERA may extend without 51% if the developer shows the attempts made. Needs Form E, a delay explanation, a revised schedule (PERT chart), disclosure of all complaints and litigation, a notarised declaration, and up-to-date QPRs and annual reports ([dearsociety](https://www.dearsociety.in/post/2025/08/28/maharera-project-extension-guide/); [ReraOne](https://reraone.co.in/blogs-and-news/maharera-project-extension-risks-requirements-remedies)). Discretionary: "not a right but a privilege". |
| QPR default | **File every missing QPR** | Forms 1, 2, 3 and the sold/unsold statement for each missed quarter, pay the ₹50,000 penalty, then MahaRERA restores status ([Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/invest/maharera-flags-8212-projects-for-missing-qpr-deadline-gives-regulatory-warning)) |
| Recently lapsed | **Check the automatic 4-month extension** | Order 66/2026 (7 Aug 2026, West Asia force majeure) adds 4 months to projects whose completion date falls on or after 28 Feb 2026, without an application ([Free Press Journal](https://freepressjournal.in/mumbai/maharera-grants-automatic-4-month-extension-to-eligible-housing-projects-citing-force-majeure-amid-west-asia-conflict)). Some leads may already be partly covered. |

Government fees are small: a convenience fee of ₹700 on an extension form (2023 order; check current) plus the Rule 7(3) extension fee (**amount unconfirmed**) ([Housing.com](https://housing.com/news/maharera-hikes-convenience-fee)). The real cost today is the developer's time and their CA or consultant.

## 5. What we pitch

**One line:** "We get your project out of MahaRERA abeyance: we find out exactly why it's stuck, prepare and file everything on MahaCRITI, and keep it compliant afterwards so it never happens again."

1. **Free diagnosis** (the hook): we read their project's MahaRERA page and tell them, in writing, why it's in abeyance and what exactly is needed (completion upload, Section 6 or 7(3) extension, missing QPRs).
2. **Restoration** (paid, fixed fee): we prepare every document and form, coordinate the architect/engineer/CA signatures, file on MahaCRITI and follow up until the project is restored.
3. **Ongoing compliance** (the subscription, and later the software): quarterly reports filed on time, deadline alerts, and later demand letters and buyer collections from the same milestone record (the wedge from `/ideas/dev-milestone`).

**Who to talk to:** the managing director / partner who decides, the authorised signatory who files on MahaRERA, or the project's CA, who certifies Form 3 and is often the one doing the filings. Never the sales desk.

**Why they'd listen:** the problem is specific, public and costly (sales, money and marketing frozen), we name it precisely, and the first step is free.
