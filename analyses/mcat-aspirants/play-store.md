## Insights

**Main finding: there is no CARS app on Android, and MCAT prep in general barely exists there.** The biggest MCAT apps are tiny: Varsity Tutors MCAT (50K+, 409 ratings, last updated 2024), Pre Medical Mastery (50K+, 321), MedSchoolCoach (50K+, 210, 3.8★, last updated 2023), Magoosh (50K+, 123, 2023), UWorld (10K+, 48), Princeton Review flashcards (10K+, 25, 2023). **Several are abandoned** (no update since 2023), and none is CARS-specific. Searching "MCAT CARS" and "reading comprehension practice" only surfaced general reading apps: Reading Trainer speed reading (100K+, 3.7★, 24% all-time 1★), "I Read: Reading Comprehension" (500K+), and children's reading apps (Reading.com, Readability). Noise: "MCat" (a live-streaming app), Indian NEET PG prep, paramedic prep. Flashcard tools (Quizlet 50M+, Knowt 500K+) are where premeds actually study on mobile, mostly science memorisation via decks.

**What this means for CARS:** CARS practice happens on desktop (AAMC CARS question packs and diagnostic, Jack Westin daily passages, UWorld, Blueprint). No one owns a mobile "10 minutes a day" CARS habit. CARSBooster's new Meta ads ("game-based CARS practice… 10 minutes a day") are aiming at exactly this gap.

**Complaints on the general reading apps** point to what a CARS trainer must avoid: paywalls on features that used to be free (Knowt, 👍316), poor speech/voice features (Readability, from a reading specialist), and "speed reading" promises that don't hold (Reading Trainer's high all-time 1★ share).
