## Insights

**CARS is a big YouTube topic.** About 30 of the 47 videos are CARS-specific. The general reading-comprehension video "How to CRUSH Reading Comprehension: 3 Effective Strategies" (5.8 lakh views, 751 comments) and "How to Study for CARS | My 9 tips for a 99th percentile score (131)" (2.1 lakh) lead. Then come many passage walkthroughs by high scorers (Shemmassian, IFD, Eric Sun, Jack Westin) and score-jump stories ("How I Went from 124 to 130 on CARS", "Improved My CARS Score by 8 Points", "increased… by 6 points (in 2 months)"). General "how I scored 520+" study-plan videos get the most comments overall (385–488).

**What works for creators:** walkthroughs of real AAMC passages by 131–132 scorers, "mistakes that are tanking your score", and timing charts. Jack Westin's own channel gets views (1.2 lakh on its top-tips video) but almost no comments (0–9).

**What commenters say about CARS / reading**
- **Reading slower to answer faster:** "'Slowest to read, fastest to answer' was exactly what I needed to hear out loud!" (👍653); "reading the passage slowly and comprehending it as i progress… got a perf[ect score]" (👍547). The idea is counterintuitive and changes behaviour.
- Strategy over volume: the advice that resonates is method (main idea, author's tone, process of elimination), not "do more passages", which matches Blueprint's "CARS isn't about reading. It's a strategy game".
- Study-plan anxiety: "Other YouTubers stress the shit outta me for no reason" (👍857).
- Timing shows up in 522 comment mentions (finishing passages, pacing charts).

**Takeaway:** aspirants want a **method taught through worked examples** by someone who scored 131–132, applied to real AAMC-style passages. Free YouTube walkthroughs are the main competition for any paid CARS product.
