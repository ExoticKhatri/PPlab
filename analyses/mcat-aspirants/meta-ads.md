## Insights

**Verdict: Meta reaches MCAT aspirants well, and CARS is a recognised sub-market.** 951 ads, all English. Reading the relevant advertisers, about **45 sell MCAT prep or med-school admissions**. The rule tag "sells to the group" (111) includes diabetes and wellness noise ("Blood Sugar Journal"), and AAMC matches AAMCO car repair. **Neither Jack Westin nor CARS-specialist tutors run notable Meta ads**; the "Jack Westin" keyword returned 3 unrelated ads.
