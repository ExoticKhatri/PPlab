## Insights

- **Getting paid on time is a live issue:** around "msme", the rising searches are **"msme 45 days payment rule" (Breakout)** and **"msme payment rule" (+2,800%)**. That is the rule (Income-tax Act section 43B(h)) under which buyers must pay registered MSMEs within 45 days or lose the tax deduction. Other risers: "jsw one msme" (Breakout, steel procurement), "pnb digi msme" (Breakout, digital loans), "msme zero cost" (+1,950%, the ZED certification scheme).
- **"udyam registration" +9% YoY** (peak 100) and steady "msme" (0%): MSME registration continues at a high level. Rising: "udyam certificate download pdf", "forgot udyam registration". These are badges needed for loans and payment protection.
- **"indiamart" -18% YoY** but still huge (average 61). Rising: "indiamart affiliate program" (+2,450%).
- **"tally prime" -11%**, but around "erp software" the risers are Tally Prime (+500%), Odoo (+180%) and KL ERP (+750%). For small firms, "ERP" mostly means Tally; Odoo is the open-source challenger.
- "erp software", "inventory management software" and "production planning" are too small to measure next to these terms. "job work" in Trends is dominated by "work from home" jobs, so ignore it.
- **Regions:** ERP and Tally interest leads in Gujarat, Maharashtra and Tamil Nadu (plus the Dadra/Daman industrial belt). IndiaMART leads in Delhi and Gujarat. These are the manufacturing heartlands; start there.
