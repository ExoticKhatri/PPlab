## Insights

**Coverage:** 150 places, 25 each in Ludhiana, Rajkot, Coimbatore, Pune, Faridabad and Surat. Every search "hit the limit", so these hubs hold far more. 1,370 reviews were read (10 lowest-rated per place), 812 with text. Collected in **19 min 46 s with 3 tabs** (vs 63 min for 200 places in one tab in the hospitals run).

**Who they are:** Manufacturer (70), pharmaceutical (10), clothing (6), auto parts (4), machining (4), plastic injection moulding (3), steel, pumps, textiles (Surat). A few big names slip in (JCB, Kohler, Texmo) next to small units. **84% have a website** (Rajkot 96%, Faridabad 72%), 95% list a phone, and owners reply to 30% of reviews. The median is only 40 reviews per place: B2B factories get few Google reviews, and many are about **wholesale buyers and walk-in customers**, not the owner's operations.

**What customers complain about** (338 reviews at ≤2★ with text; keyword themes, overlapping)
- Delivery / dispatch / order mix-ups: 47 (14%). "I ordered a total of 22 pieces, But none of my ordered dresses were delivered, Everything I received was totally different except 1 model"; "still waiting for LR number… E-way bill was gene[rated]" (Surat textiles).
- No response to calls/emails: 47 (14%). "wrote a mail and no response… marked In Transit for…" (Kohler).
- Product quality / defects: 43 (13%). "the pump failed during the initial trial" (Kalbro, Faridabad); "the motor is producing erroneous and disturbing noises" (Texmo).
- Employees and work culture: 41 (12%); salary/PF/wages specifically: 11.
- Refunds / advance payments / "cheated": 24 (7%). "I purchased stock of around 1.7 lakh they are not refunding my money" (Parnika, Surat).
- Wholesale vs retail confusion: "expecting a wholesale experience based on their social media presence, but I found that the pricing is strictly retail" (Kesaria, Surat; two similar reviews).

**Takeaway:** Maps confirms the group is large, dense in each hub, and online (websites and phones). Its reviews mostly reflect **order fulfilment problems (wrong items, dispatch status, no response)** at small units that sell to traders. That's an order-to-dispatch tracking and communication gap, the same "do you know what's happening in your factory?" pain the ERP vendors advertise on Meta. Reviews say little about owners' internal problems (finance, labour), so use YouTube, Meta and Trends for those.
