# Outreach messages: late-payment research

Use these with the contact sheet (`analyses/msme-leads/raw/google-maps/leads.csv`, local only) and the [interview script](interview-script.md). Replace everything in [brackets].

**Order for each contact:**
1. **Email** (where the sheet has one).
2. **Call** 1–2 days later, mentioning the email.
3. **WhatsApp** only after they've agreed to talk, or as a polite follow-up that mentions the email or call.

**Trust rules (they separate you from scammers):**
- Use your real name, a real email signature, and a phone number that answers.
- Mention something specific about them (what they make, their city).
- Say clearly that you are not selling anything, and won't ask for money, OTPs or documents.
- No links in a first message. Don't ask for invoices until they offer a next step.
- Keep it short. Scams are long and urgent; this should be short and calm.

---

## 1. First email

**Subject:** Quick question about payments from your buyers, [Company name]

> Dear [Name / Sir or Madam],
>
> I'm [Your name], and I'm researching how small manufacturers in [City] deal with late payments from their buyers. I came across [Company name] while looking at [auto parts / CNC job work / moulding…] suppliers in [City].
>
> I'm not selling anything. I'm talking to around 15 factory owners to understand how often payments are delayed, what it costs the business, and what you do about it today. I won't ask for any payment, OTP or documents.
>
> Would you have 5 minutes for a short call this week? Please let me know a time that suits you, or simply reply here.
>
> Thank you,
> [Your name]
> [Your role / Company, e.g. "Independent researcher" or "Founder, [Company]"]
> [Phone number] · [Email]
> [LinkedIn profile URL]

---

## 2. Follow-up email (3–4 days later, if no reply)

**Subject:** Re: Quick question about payments from your buyers, [Company name]

> Dear [Name],
>
> Just following up on my note from [day]. I'm speaking with small manufacturers about late payments from buyers, and your experience would really help. It takes 5 minutes, and I'm happy to share a short summary of what other owners told me.
>
> If now isn't a good time, no problem at all.
>
> Best regards,
> [Your name] · [Phone number]

---

## 3. Phone call opener (after the email)

> Hello, is this [Name / the owner of Company name]? My name is [Your name]. I sent you an email on [day] about research on late payments from buyers. I'm not selling anything. Could you spare 5 minutes now, or is there a better time?

- **If yes:** continue with the [interview script](interview-script.md), section 2.
- **If they didn't see the email:** "No problem. I'm talking to small manufacturers in [City] about how often buyers pay late and what it costs them. Just 5 minutes."
- **If busy:** "Of course. When should I call back?" Note the time in the sheet.
- **If the number is wrong or it's reception:** "Could you tell me who handles accounts or payments? May I have their number?"

---

## 4. WhatsApp follow-up (only after an email or call)

**After a call where they agreed to talk later:**
> Hello [Name], this is [Your name]. We spoke earlier about my research on late payments from buyers. As agreed, I'll call you on [day] at [time]. Thank you for your time.

**After an email with no reply (send once, never repeat):**
> Hello [Name], I'm [Your name]. I emailed you on [day] about research on how small manufacturers deal with late payments from buyers. I'm not selling anything and won't ask for any payment, OTP or documents. Would a 5-minute call this week be possible?

---

## 5. Thank-you message (after a conversation)

> Thank you for your time today, [Name]. Your experience with [the delayed payment from X / 90-day payments] was very helpful. Once I've spoken to a few more owners, I'll share a short summary of what I learned. If you know another owner who faces the same problem, I'd be grateful for an introduction.

---

## Tracking

In the contact sheet, fill in `status` (emailed, called, replied, interviewed, declined, wrong number) and `notes`. Copy the answers from each conversation into the scorecard in the [interview script](interview-script.md).

**Pace:** about 15 emails a day, then calls. **Stop target:** 5 interviews that confirm the pain, or 50 contacts tried, whichever comes first.
