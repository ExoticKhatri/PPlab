## Insights

**Caveat:** discovery again kept the most-reviewed apps, so LinkedIn, Naukri, Alibaba, Todoist, Asana and monday.com fill much of the list. Manufacturing ERPs (TranZact, Biziverse, Rednote…) are web/desktop and don't appear. The factory-relevant apps are below.

**Relevant apps**
- **B2B marketplaces (how factories find buyers):** IndiaMART (10M+, 4.8★, 8 lakh ratings), udaan (10M+, B2B for retailers), TradeIndia (1M+, 3.9★), JdMart (500K+, 4.4★), Alibaba.
- **Staff attendance and payroll (factory labour):** PagarBook (10M+, 4.4★), SalaryBox (1M+), Workex (1M+, 3.3★), FactoHR (500K+), Attendance Register (5M+).
- **Billing / inventory:** Vyapar, myBillBook (10M+ each), Zoho Invoice, Tally mobile, GimBooks, Swipe, StoDo inventory (1M+).

**What sellers on B2B marketplaces complain about** (4,000 reviews on IndiaMART, udaan, TradeIndia and JdMart; 1,174 complaints)
- **Paid packages that don't deliver: 379.** "I get two package 35000 + 20000 before 6 month but I not get right queries, all queries are unverified and by mistake leads" (JdMart, 👍83); "all leads are fake, they are charging for fake leads… I requested refund" (JdMart, 👍76); "To become a seller you need to buy buyleads for 40,000. Then only you can contact the buyer. Receiving spam calls daily to buy buyleads" (IndiaMART); "At the time of taking the payment they told something else and after receiving the payment they said something else" (IndiaMART).
- Spam calls: 120. Support: 122. Fake/irrelevant leads named explicitly: 90.
- IndiaMART itself is rated well (4.8★, 11.5% complaints in the latest 1,000 reviews). Most complaints are from sellers about paid lead packages.

**What breaks in staff/payroll apps** (3,000 reviews, 852 complaints): login/crash 100, subscriptions and auto-pay 64, attendance (location/selfie) 61, salary/overtime/advance calculations 50. "when I open my staff loan details it was there… in the evening… nothing was there, it makes me shocked and worry about the data" (PagarBook, 👍38); "there is no option to unsubscribe" auto-pay (Attendance Register).

**Takeaway:** on mobile, small manufacturers mainly **find buyers** (IndiaMART and similar, paying ₹20,000–55,000 for lead packages and often unhappy with lead quality) and **manage workers** (attendance, advances, overtime). Production and inventory software lives elsewhere.
