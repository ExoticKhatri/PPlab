## Insights

- **"mba" +44% YoY in Singapore** (peak 100, average 52 vs 36). Interest in MBAs is rising sharply. Rising related searches: "online mba programs" (Breakout), "mba programs" (+1,900%), "mba online" (+1,200%), "mba 是 什么" (+180%, Chinese-speaking audience). Part of the rise is online and part-time MBAs.
- **GMAT-specific risers (all Breakout):** "gmat focus edition", "gmat focus vs gmat", **"ttp gmat"**, "gmat focus score calculator", **"is gre easier than gmat"**. The Focus Edition is still being figured out, Target Test Prep is the rising brand, and some applicants are weighing a switch to the GRE.
- **Around "gmat prep":** "target test prep" +400%, "target test prep gmat" +300%, "magoosh" +60%. Manhattan Prep is a top term but not rising.
- **"insead" -6%** (stable, average 4.2). Rising: "insead global executive mba" (+140%), "insead mim" (+120%).
- **Executive MBA:** "quantic executive mba" +350% and "online executive mba" +60%. Cheaper online EMBAs are gaining.
- **"gre" +49% is not real for this group:** its rising searches are AMD graphics cards ("RX 7900 GRE", "RX 9070 GRE").
- GMAT terms themselves are too small next to "mba" to show % change; Singapore is a small market, so absolute volumes are low.
