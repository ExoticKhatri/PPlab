## Insights

**GMAT prep on Android is small and dominated by three names:** GMAT Club Forum (100K+, 5.0★, 2,908 ratings), **Magoosh** (GMAT Prep 100K+, 4.4★, IAP $369.98–$499.99; Math Flashcards 100K+; Idiom Flashcards 100K+, last updated 2023), and Manhattan Prep GMAT 2026 (100K+, 4.2★, 983 ratings, IAP $13.99–$149.98). Others are near-empty (Varsity Tutors GMAT, TCY MBA prep with 0 ratings). The rest of the 12 apps are focus/pomodoro timers (Focus Friend, Focus Quest, Focusmeter, Study Time Tracker), pulled in by "GMAT **Focus**". The Singapore store has **no GMAT Focus–specific, Data Insights–specific or verbal-specific app**.

**Takeaway:** mobile GMAT prep is a thin market run by two big brands' companion apps plus the GMAT Club community app. Like MCAT and LSAT, real practice happens on the desktop (official GMAC practice, TTP, e-GMAT, GMAT Club). Mobile is for flashcards and forum browsing. The many focus-timer apps (and their reviews) show that **staying consistent while studying around a full-time job** is itself a pain this audience pays to address.
