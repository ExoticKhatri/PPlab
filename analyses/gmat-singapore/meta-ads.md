## Insights

**Verdict: in Singapore, Meta is a business-school market, not a GMAT-prep market.** 858 ads from 194 advertisers. Most are **MBA and Executive MBA programmes**: INSEAD Executive Education (88 ads, 213 days), INSEAD (62), Nanyang Business School (35), IE (22), IMD (20), HKUST, Wharton, NUS MBA/EMBA, SMU, Oxford Saïd, Stern at NYUAD, HBS, plus many online-MBA sellers (IIBM, Swiss/UK online MBAs) and MBA fairs (AccessMBA: "The best business schools are coming to Singapore on November 2nd", QS Discover MBA at Suntec). Keyword volumes reflect this: "Executive MBA" 166 ads, INSEAD 161, MBA 150, MBA Singapore 148, against "GMAT prep" 28, "GMAT tutor" 6, "GMAT course" 0, "GMAT class" 2, "GMAT Singapore" 2.

**GMAT-prep advertisers are almost absent:**
- **GMAC** (official, 58 ads, 204 days): "Master the GMAT with official guides, real practice questions, and mock exams — everything you need in one premium bundle."
- **Magoosh** (38 ads, 365 days): mostly GRE prep.
- **INSEAD** (12 ads): "INSEAD's curated ultimate guide for choosing the right GMAT or GRE test". The school itself educates applicants on the test.
- **"GMAT Verbal"** (2 ads, 8 days), at gmatverbal.niopractice.com: "GMAT Verbal – hit V86+ in less than 200 Practice Questions… 100 Critical Reasoning, then 100 Reading Compr[ehension]". The only verbal-specific GMAT advertiser seen.
- Generic tutors listing GMAT among many tests (Think Global, Manhattan Review vocabulary flashcards).
- **No Target Test Prep, e-GMAT, Manhattan Prep or Kaplan GMAT advertisers** appeared as sellers in Singapore (the keyword hits were incidental).

**Language:** almost all English, with a few Chinese and Korean MBA-consulting ads (cross-border applicants).

**Takeaway:** Singapore's MBA *demand* side is heavily advertised (schools spend to recruit), while the GMAT *prep* side is **nearly uncontested on Meta**. A prep product would advertise into an audience that business schools are already warming up, with little competition for the same attention.
