## Insights

**All values are relative to "gst software" as the anchor. The niche terms are too small for Trends to measure.** "practice management software", "gst reconciliation", "ca firm" and "tds software" all show "too little data". That doesn't mean nobody searches; it means volumes are small next to mass terms like "tally prime" and "itr filing".

- **Tally Prime dwarfs everything** (peak 100, 12-month average 60) and is roughly flat (-9% YoY). Tally is the installed base CA firms work inside, so any new tool has to work with Tally. Rising related searches are all Tally version numbers (6.1, 6.2, 7.1) and "edit log" (the audit-trail feature): an upgrade-and-compliance cycle, not new adoption.
- **"gst software" +14% and "income tax software" +26% YoY:** growing, but from small bases (averages 4.7 and 2.8 vs Tally's 60).
- **"itr filing" -37% YoY:** a seasonal spike term (peak 100 in July). The drop is likely season timing or a smaller peak this year. Weekly data is too noisy to read as decline.
- **Rising around "gst software":** swipe billing software (Breakout), mybillbook (+3,500%), speqta gst (+2,750%), gst doctor software (+1,550%). Cheap or free SME billing apps are growing fast. These are the apps CAs' clients use.
- **Rising around "practice management software":** "zoho practice" (Breakout), "zoho practice management software" (Breakout), "icai practice management software" (+400%), "tax practice management software" (+250%). **Zoho has entered CA practice management, and ICAI is promoting its own tool.** Both are big, cheap or free competitors to watch.
- **"tds software":** top related searches are download, price, free and "saral tds software" (price-led again).

**Regions:** GST software leads in Gujarat, Delhi, Tamil Nadu and Chandigarh. Income-tax software leads in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. Practice-management searches spread across Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Maharashtra, Karnataka and Delhi. "ca firm" leads in Delhi and Maharashtra. For a first market, the evidence points to Gujarat, Delhi NCR, Maharashtra and the South (Telangana/AP/TN).
