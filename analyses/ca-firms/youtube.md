## Insights

**Caveat: most of this data is not practising CAs.** Of 71 videos and 15,670 comments, the biggest videos are about CA exams and career (CA Wallah, Unacademy, "How to prepare for CA Foundation"), plus US/general videos ("What do accountants actually do?", TEDx, Gen Z) and B.Com finance lectures. I kept **45 practice-related videos (13.7 lakh views, 2,088 comments)** for the analysis below. Even there, many commenters are students and articled assistants.

**What draws views (interest)**
- **Learning compliance work:** the GST filing course (GSTR-1/2B/3B, 4.6 lakh views, 480 comments) is the biggest practice video by far. Commenters are accountants and juniors learning returns: "jab bhi mai kahi interview Dene jata hu woh ye puchte hai ki apko return filing aati hai kya" (they keep getting asked in interviews whether they know return filing).
- **Job vs practice, and income:** "CA in Job vs CA in Practice" (2.5 lakh), "1 Crore income for CA" (20K).
- **Getting clients:** "How I win clients in my CA practice" (1.1 lakh), "Client Hunting in 2026" (82K), "I Got 10 NEW Clients in 15 Days" (16K).
- **Automation with AI:** "Automate Ledger Scrutiny in Tally with Claude AI" (65K views, June 2026). This is new and getting traction.
- **Software demos get almost no engagement:** Practive (6.7K views, 4 comments), CA OMS (3,223 views, 0 comments), Hidesc (2,469 views, 0), Practicely (1,360, 1), "Practice Management Software … Offline | No Subscription" (886, 4). **At least 6 vendors are making practice-management demo videos, and almost nobody discusses them.**

**Recurring themes in the 2,088 practice-video comments** (keyword themes, overlapping)
- GST/ITR/TDS/audit compliance work: 253 (mostly the GST course)
- Software/tools/automation/AI: 131
- Income and earnings: 126
- Starting a practice / first clients: 101
- Getting clients / marketing: 98
- Staff and articles (stipends, hiring): 86
- Fees, pricing and payment collection: 80

**In their own words**
- Starting is a long struggle: "Practice is sooooo good if we dare to have patience and enjoy the sacrifices including rejection & empty pockets..." (👍58); "I think first 2 to 3 years you have to make struggle after it you have so many clients" (👍41)
- **Payment collection:** "Collection of Payment from Client is a Major Task.....Otherwise to do tasks and Assignments or Projects is not [hard]" (👍17, a tax consultant 3 years into practice)
- Pricing pressure: "Never fight on prices" (👍11)
- Client acquisition is constrained by ICAI rules: "A CA in practice is prohibited to write any message/letter e-mail for solicitation of work" (👍23)
- Firms run on cheap articled labour: "When most of their work is being done by CA articles with very cheap stipend then obviously they will have 50% of profit margins" (👍66); "Every CA firms have one business tactics in common : juice out the people for articleship" (👍30)
- Small clients are going in-house or DIY: "now a days every firm either small or large have their own lawyer or accountant… who did everything like auditing gst registration" (👍13)
- Tier-3 practices: "How a CA practice based in tier 3 city can compete with those in big cities" (👍8)
- Interest in AI tooling: a podcast idea asks about "CAs in practice… who you think have cracked custom GPTs and offline LLMs" (👍26)

**Takeaway:** YouTube shows that practising CAs mostly worry about **getting clients, pricing and collecting fees, and staffing with cheap articles**. Software comes up mainly as "automation/AI", and practice-management demos get no discussion. A tool pitched as "practice management" would be entering a category CAs don't talk about. Payment collection and client/document chasing are what they do complain about.
