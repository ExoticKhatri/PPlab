## Insights

**Main finding: CA practice software is not on the Play Store.** None of the 40 apps is a practice-management or professional tax tool for CA firms (Winman, Genius, CompuOffice, KDK, Gen and Saral are Windows desktop software). App discovery therefore pulled in neighbours and noise. Treat this file as evidence about the *clients* of CA firms and about DIY tax apps, not about CAs' own tools.

**What the 40 apps are**
- **Noise (11):** ixigo, Porter, DigiLocker, Landeed, stock-trading apps (mStock, Dhan, Univest, Religare, Lemonn), and US apps (IRS, TaxSlayer). The search terms "tally" and "itr filing" are too broad on Play. Exclude these next time.
- **SME billing/accounting apps used by CAs' clients (10):** Vyapar (10M+), myBillBook (10M+), Khatabook (100M+), Zoho Books (5M+, IAP up to ₹23,599), GimBooks (5M+), Swipe, Tally mobile (500K+, IAP ₹60–1,500), Xero, Accounting Bookkeeping, GST Invoice Billing. All are free to download, with paid plans inside.
- **DIY or assisted ITR filing, which competes with CAs for small taxpayers (5):** ClearTax/Black (500K+), TaxBuddy (1M+), All India ITR (1M+), myITreturn (1M+, rated 2.7), IndiaFilings (500K+).
- **Government tax apps (2):** AIS for Taxpayers (3.8), Aaykar Setu (3.6, 50% complaints).
- **Other (9):** GST and tax calculators (4), Income Tax Act reference, IRIS Peridot (GST + MSME loans), Refrens invoicing, Entri (job courses), Tally Counter (a click counter matched by "tally").
- **ICAI (3):** ICAI (2.2★, last updated Sept 2024), ICAI BOS, and an EduRev CA Foundation prep app. These are mostly students.

**Who is speaking:** only 504 of 38,641 reviews (1.3%) mention a CA or accountant, and 330 of those are on the ICAI and CA-exam apps (students). The practising-CA voice is almost absent here, just like the school-admin voice in the K-12 run.

**Complaints about the accounting apps CAs' clients use** (1,935 complaints in 10,000 reviews of the 10 billing/accounting apps; keyword themes, overlapping):
- Price / paywall / subscription: 409 (21%). "The basic features in inventory, they are giving only in advance versions" (Zoho Books, 👍416). "bought this junk for 17K for their professional team and had to buy another software to print inv[oices]" (Zoho Books, 👍324).
- Support: 285 (15%)
- Missing features / can't edit: 265 (14%). "the customer or vendor cannot be changed once a voucher [is saved]" (Zoho Books, 👍256). "Split payments (part cash, part online) aren't tracked acc[urately]" (Vyapar, 👍407).
- Crashes / slow / login: 247 (13%)
- Reports (P&L, ledger, balance sheet): 82 (4%)
- Bank entries typed by hand: "there should be an option to import Bank statement as there are 200-300 monthly Bank entries which we have to feed manually" (Accounting Bookkeeping, 2★)

**Signals relevant to CA firms**
- Clients' data lives in many different apps (Vyapar, myBillBook, Khatabook, Zoho, Tally). A CA must pull it all into Tally or tax software. This matches the Meta ads for AI "bills/bank statement to Tally" tools.
- The good outcome clients describe: "easy during tax filing as our CA could easily browse through the Invoices & Credit notes generated which are saved in the cloud" (Zoho Books, 👍201).
- DIY tax apps fail when a human is needed: "The expert assigned is not even a CA" (TaxBuddy, 👍51); "even if you opt for CA by paying extra amount. very poor customer service" (myITreturn, 👍50). For simple returns these apps are the CA's competition, but they lose trust once things get complex.
