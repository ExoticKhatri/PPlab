## Insights

**Published:** **SimplerQMS: from USD 17,500/year** (≈$1,460/month) for up to 15 users; Techjockey lists a Starter at USD 1,150. This is the only published price.

**Demo-led (no public price):** MasterControl, Qualio, QT9, Scilife, ComplianceQuest, Dot Compliance, and the Indian vendors AmpleLogic and Caliber.

**Price band:** an eQMS for a small life-sciences company costs **≈$1,000–1,500/month** at the entry level. Enterprise systems (MasterControl, Veeva) are far higher and quote-only. Indian vendors (AmpleLogic, Caliber) sell to Indian plants on quotes; their prices are likely lower but are not public.
