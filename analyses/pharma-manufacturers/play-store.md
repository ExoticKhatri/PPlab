## Insights

**Off-target.** 289 candidate apps were found and 35 kept. They are pharmacy and retail apps (Truemeds, Pharmarack, eVitalRx, Marg PharmaNxt), clinical guideline apps (ESC, NCCN), pharma learning apps (Pharmaguideline, Pharma Sapience), and IPO "GMP" apps, where GMP means "grey market premium" in finance. The only plant-adjacent tool is GoAudits (inspections, 100K+, 4.8★). Plants run QMS, LIMS and batch records on desktop and web systems, so Play has no meaningful competitor or complaint data.

**Takeaway:** Play Store is not a useful source for this group. The pain signal is on YouTube, Google and Reddit.
