## Insights

**Coverage:** 132 firms in Mumbai, Chennai, Delhi, Ahmedabad and Kolkata (every search hit its cap). Categories: freight forwarding agency (53), customs broker (44), logistics service (17).

**Digitally mature:** 89% have a website, 95% list a phone, median 26 reviews, and owners reply to 37%.

**What clients complain about** (164 reviews at ≤2★, mostly importers, exporters and individuals sending goods): **no response / no updates 57**, delays 22, customs problems 21, **extra or hidden charges 18**, fraud claims 16, damage or loss 9.
- "They lure you with low quotes, then double the charges post-service."
- "you'll be liable to pay charges in lakhs due to their misinformation."
- "they charged me 77% [duty] for some small toy gift imported from korea, they don't have customer support not even responding to my queries."

**Takeaway:** forwarders' clients complain most about **communication and surprise charges**: what is happening to the shipment, and what it will really cost. That matches the Google status searches (bill of entry status, detention free days) and the Reddit request for transparency.
