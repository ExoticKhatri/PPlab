## Insights

**Mostly the wrong market:** Play results are trucking and courier apps (Porter 50M+, Wheelseye, DTDC, package trackers, US load boards) and vessel trackers (MarineTraffic, ShipAtlas). 15 apps were dropped as irrelevant. Forwarding software is desktop and web based: CargoWise, Logi-Sys and Magaya have no meaningful Play presence. Only a few company ERPs (VIL ERP, NWAY ERP) and container tracking (Lynktrac, SeaRates) appear.

**Takeaway:** Play Store is not a useful source for this group. Complaints about trucking and courier apps belong to other markets (shippers and drivers).
