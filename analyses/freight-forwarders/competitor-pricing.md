## Insights

**Only one vendor publishes a price:** **Shipthis**, $89 per user per month for all modules (shipment and freight management, quotations, freight accounting, warehouse). That is ≈₹7,500 per user per month.

**Demo-led (no public price):** Logi-Sys, CargoWise, Magaya, Freightify, GoFreight (Techjockey: "price on request"); Freightbro and Cogoport show no pricing. The Freightos page read was its container price index (FBX), not product pricing, so ignore those numbers. Reddit adds that CargoWise moved to a "Value Pack" model with an **automation fee charged per transaction**.

**Price band:** per-user pricing is the norm. A 5-person forwarder on Shipthis would pay ≈₹37,500/month (estimate from the published $89). CargoWise and Logi-Sys are enterprise-priced and quote-only.
