## Insights

**Mostly forwarders advertising themselves:** 520 ads from 303 advertisers. Most are forwarders and CHAs selling services to importers ("China to Delhi door-to-door"), logistics courses (Kerala institutes), and noise. Real sellers *to* forwarders:

| Category | Advertisers (approx.) | Examples | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Forwarding / logistics software | ~3 | Avaal Freight Management "Bharat" (525 days), InvoIdea (white-label logistics platform), courier software (ESE CRM) | **Thin** |
| Rate marketplaces and lead platforms | ~4 | FindRates AI (22 ads: "Submit your shipment once and receive verified offers"), Globy ("175,000+ global traders… send us your freight rates"), All Forward | Busy |
| APIs for logistics software | 1 | Masters India (e-way bill and FASTag APIs) | Thin |
| Courses and careers | ~50 | Frontline Academy, Shipsol 360, Route Upskill | Busy (the jobs market, not buyers) |

**Can Meta reach forwarders?** Weakly. Software vendors (CargoWise, Logi-Sys, Magaya) don't advertise there. Forwarders do advertise, so they use Meta to win shipper leads, which makes them buyers of lead and rate tools rather than of software.
