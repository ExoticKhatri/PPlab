## Insights

**Coverage:** 119 places in 6 cities (20 each, every search "hit the limit"). 101 are LSAT/test-prep or tutoring businesses: 80 "Tutoring service", 11 educational consultants, 4 test-prep centres. 955 reviews with text were read, 10 lowest-rated per place.

**Market shape:** in-person LSAT prep is a tutoring-company market. Chains are few and small on Maps: Princeton Review (6 locations), Odyssey LSAT (6), Advantage Testing (4), Blueprint (3), Manhattan Elite (3), Manhattan Prep (3). 98% have websites. Ratings are high (4.85 average, median 33 reviews), so low reviews are a small but telling minority. Owners reply to 38% of them.
