## Insights

**Main finding: LSAT prep barely happens on Android.** Only 6 of the 30 apps are LSAT-specific, and they're small:

| App | Downloads | Rating (ratings) | Last updated |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LSATMax (TestMax) | 100K+ | 4.7 (918) | Apr 2025 |
| 7Sage LSAT | 10K+ | 4.4 (227) | Oct 2026 |
| LSAT Prep 2026 (Fast Prep) | 10K+ | 4.3 (46) | Aug 2026 |
| LSAT Prep & Practice (Magoosh) | 10K+ | 4.0 (34) | Aug 2026 |
| LSAT Prep 2026 (PassExam) | 10K+ | 3.6 (27) | May 2026 |
| LSAT Games (7Sage) | 1K+ | 4.5 (21) | Oct 2026 |

There is **no LawHub app**, even though LawHub is the official test interface. The real test is taken on a computer, so prep lives on the web. The rest of the 30 apps are generic flashcards (Quizlet 50M+, Anki 10M+, Brainscape), law-reference apps and "logical reasoning" quiz apps for other exams. 13 were filtered out (other tests, a game).

**Complaints on the 6 LSAT apps** (87 of 244 reviews are ≤3★; keyword themes):
- **Broken app vs web, sync and login: 26.** "I already paid for my access to LSATMAX on my computer… it's saying I never paid for access?" (👍24); "if I try to sign in on my Samsung S8, It crashes" (👍30); 7Sage: "Not as good as iPhone version… cannot pick up where you left off" (👍11), "Nearly 4 years since an update? Logic games tab crashes the app" (👍4).
- **Paywalls and pricing tricks: 18.** "An immediate paywall… and the app had several spelling mistakes on the first page" (PassExam); "It says the price is 5.99 per week, but when I click it, it goes to 8 per week… deceptive pricing" (Fast Prep).
- **Content quality: 11.** "logic test is contaning huge mistakes" (LSATMax, 👍56); "I do not think these questions are well aligned to actual LSAC materials" (KP).
- Many complaints are about **Logic Games**, a section the LSAT removed in August 2024. Older apps are now partly out of date.

**Takeaway:** mobile is an open but small niche, used for short drills, review and flashcards. The incumbent apps are thin companions to web courses, and no official app exists.
