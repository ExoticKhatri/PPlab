## Insights

- **"law school" +28% and "law school admissions" +37% YoY:** interest in going to law school is rising clearly. **"lsat" is up a modest +6%.** This fits the record applicant pools that Reddit and the tutor ads talk about ("LSAT MEDIANS ARE HIGHER THAN EVER").
- Brand and product terms (lsat prep, 7sage, lawhub, lsat tutor, lsat practice test) are too small next to "law school" for Trends to measure ("too little data"). That doesn't mean low demand; it's the anchor's scale.
- **Rising around "lsat prep":** lawhub (+2,400%), lsat demon (+1,200%), lsat diagnostic test (+200%), 7sage (+190%). **LawHub (the official platform) and LSAT Demon are the fast risers**, 7Sage is growing, and Kaplan/Princeton Review don't appear.
- **Rising around "lsat":** "lsat argumentative writing" (Breakout), a newer part of the test, plus yearly "lsat dates" searches.
- **Rising around "7sage":** "classic 7sage" (+3,900%), meaning users looking for the old version after a redesign, and "7sage fee waiver" (+250%), which signals price sensitivity.
- **Rising around "law school":** "how much is law school" (+100%), "can you take the bar without going to law school" (+120%). Cost anxiety is growing.
- **Regions:** DC leads every term by far (100), then New York, Massachusetts, Connecticut, Maryland, New Jersey and California. That is the Northeast corridor plus California. "lsat tutor vancouver" is a Breakout, a Canadian pocket.
