# LSAT aspirants (US/Canada) — group analysis

Collected 23–24 Oct 2026 across 7 platforms ([config.json](config.json)). Per-platform detail: [Meta ads](meta-ads.md) · [Play Store](play-store.md) · [YouTube](youtube.md) · [Google search](google-search.md) · [Google Trends](google-trends.md) · [Reddit](reddit.md) · [Google Maps](google-maps.md).

**Data collected:** 1,571 Meta ads (550 advertisers) · 30 apps / 8,742 Play reviews · 49 videos / 6,661 YouTube comments · 1,763 Google autocomplete searches + 6 result pages · 8 Trends terms · 1,110 Reddit posts / 2,059 comments · 119 prep businesses / 1,018 Maps reviews.

## 1. The group

People preparing for the LSAT to apply to US and Canadian law schools: mostly college seniors and young professionals, many studying while working full time. They study for months, often retake, and treat the score as the lever for admission *and* scholarships. Group size isn't measured in this data (LSAC publishes yearly test-taker counts; check those before sizing). Interest is growing: Google Trends shows "law school" +28% and "law school admissions" +37% year on year, with "lsat" +6%.

## 2. Can we reach them? Yes — easily, and cheaply via community

- **Meta:** about 33 prep/admissions sellers and about 25 law schools pay to reach them. Blueprint has run ads for 390 days (93 ads). All ads are in English.
- **Reddit is their home:** r/LSAT (252K members) and r/lawschooladmissions (277K) are very active and candid. Peer recommendations decide purchases: Google autocomplete is full of "… reddit" ("best lsat prep course reddit", "is 7sage worth it reddit").
- The two prep brands they talk about most, **7Sage and LSAT Demon, run no Meta ads**. They grow through content, podcasts and community. LawHub and LSAT Demon are the fast risers in Trends (+2,400% and +1,200%).
- YouTube reaches them with free, honest guidance from high scorers ("How I Scored a 175": 2.1 lakh views).

## 4. Competitors and prices

- **Platforms:** 7Sage (Core ~$69.95/month; most recommended), LSAT Demon (~$200/month; podcast-led, criticised for AI answers and tone), LawHub Advantage by LSAC ($120/year; the official tests and test interface), Blueprint (heaviest Meta spender), Kaplan, Princeton Review, PowerScore (books + admissions consulting), Magoosh, LSATMax. Free: Khan Academy, Loophole/Bible books, YouTube.
- **Tutors:** peers advertise $15–65/hour on Reddit. Established tutors and big companies charge $100–300/hour.
- **Mobile:** almost empty. The largest LSAT app (LSATMax) has 100K+ downloads and 918 ratings, and there is no LawHub app.
- **Routes around the LSAT:** JD-Next (an alternative admissions test), no-LSAT programs abroad, online JDs.

## 5. Opportunities spotted in the data

1. **Automatic review / wrong-answer journal on top of LawHub.** It would log every miss, tag it by question type and trap, schedule re-drills, and track plateaus. Evidence: pain #1 and #3, the ⬆151 post with "thousands" of DMs, and "You just need a service that gives full access to previous tests" (LawHub) plus good review.
   - Risk: depends on LawHub data access and LSAC's terms; 7Sage could copy it.
2. **An affordable AI tutor that explains *your* mistakes**, priced between ChatGPT ($20) and Demon/7Sage ($70–200), with official-style questions. Evidence: pains #2 and #5, the AI backlash against Demon (people want honesty, not hidden AI), and $100–300/hour tutors.
   - Risk: question licensing (LSAC owns the PrepTests); trust in AI explanations.
3. **A mobile companion for drills and review**, the gap on Play. Evidence: tiny, broken incumbents and no official app.
   - Risk: real practice happens on desktop in the LawHub interface; mobile is supplementary, so willingness to pay is low.

Go-to-market evidence: start on Reddit/YouTube and with high scorers as creators, not on Meta. The leaders win there, and aspirants search with "reddit".

## 6. Data gaps

- **Meta:** 8 US keyword searches failed (admissions, personal statement, LR/RC, LawHub), so admissions consulting is under-counted. Re-run those.
- **Reddit:** stopped at 67 of 100 threads (rate limit 429). r/OntarioLawSchools doesn't exist. Some off-topic posts leaked in through the "law school" term (collector now fixed); the analysis excludes them.
- **Play Store:** the first run's "sat " filter dropped real LSAT apps; this was fixed and re-run.
- **YouTube:** the "is law school worth it" query pulled 2 Indian law-career videos.
- **Not collected:** group size (LSAC volumes), TikTok/Instagram (where LSAT coaches advertise), Canadian-specific prep, Discord study groups.
