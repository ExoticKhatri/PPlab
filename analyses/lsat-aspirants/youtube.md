## Insights

**What draws views:** the big videos are about **whether to go to law school at all**: LegalEagle "Is Law School Worth It?" (7.8 lakh views), Business Insider (4.6 lakh), Cece Xie "no, you probably shouldn't go to law school" (3.5 lakh). Next come **personal success stories**: "How I Got Into Harvard Law School" (3.2 lakh) and "THE BEST LSAT RESOURCES | How I Scored a 175" (2.1 lakh). Prep-company strategy videos get far fewer views and comments. LSAT Demon has 90K and 47K views with 0–1 comments; 7Sage's "Master Conditional Logic" has 95K views and 31 comments. Two Indian law-career videos (Warikoo, Priya Jain) are noise from the "is law school worth it" query.

**Comments** (6,661; 1,308 on the 34 LSAT-titled videos; keyword themes, overlapping): thanks/helpful 1,004, cost/money 692, timing 522, working/job 470, law-school-worth-it/debt 443, prep companies named 155, retakes 123, score stories 113, anxiety 91.

**In their own words**
- Free resources win trust: "Thank you… You give people the information people really need, instead of selling a single study program." (👍17)
- 7Sage dominates as a recommendation: "As someone who just scored a 175… I second the 7Sage recommendation. It is an absolute life saver" (👍301). It doesn't suit everyone: "As someone with ADHD, I had a really hard time with 7sage because explaining the points turned out to 5 into 10 minute rambles for one question" (👍54).
- Timing is the core struggle: "Y'all should change the name of the podcast to the 'Stop Trying to Finish the Section' podcast." (👍87, LSAT Demon)
- Self-study is hard and lonely: "Self studying for this exam has been brutal." (👍41); "this test is ridiculously hard and trying to afford a teacher is difficult" (👍19)
- Focus and attention: "I think i gotta watch this video 3 more times to make sure i watch it without zoning out" (👍78)
- Logic Games removal is a relief: "The Analytical Reasoning/Logic Games section made me so nervous… Looking forward to [the new format]" (👍19)
- Tone matters: on a Demon episode, "way to degrade any ounce of confidence this kid had" (👍34)

**Takeaway:** aspirants want free, honest, step-by-step guidance from people who did well. They struggle with timing and focus, and many study alone while working. The "is it worth it / debt" question sits over the whole journey.
