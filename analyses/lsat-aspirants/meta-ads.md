## Insights

**Gaps:** 8 US keyword searches failed (LSAT score, law school admissions/application, pre-law, personal statement, LR, RC, LawHub), so US admissions-consulting ads are under-counted. "7Sage" and "PowerScore" pulled lots of noise ("Clara Sage", "Monica Sage", romance-fiction apps like ChapterBound). **LSAT Demon runs no ads at all** (0 in both countries), and 7Sage didn't appear as an advertiser either. The two prep brands Reddit talks about most grow through community and content, not Meta.

**Verdict: Meta reaches LSAT aspirants well.** From reading the relevant advertisers, about **33 prep/admissions sellers** and **~25 law schools** are paying to reach them. All ads are in English (100%).
