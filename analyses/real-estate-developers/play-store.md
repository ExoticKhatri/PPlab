## Insights

**Landscape:** construction apps lead. Onsite (1M+, 6,750 ratings) and Powerplay (1M+, 2,376, 4.7★) are Indian; Buildertrend, Fieldwire and Raken are global. Indian real estate CRMs are small on Play: Buildesk (10K+, 338 ratings), Leadrat Lite (10K+), Site CRM, PropFlo, DaeBuild and others with under 50 ratings. Developers run CRMs on the web, so the app numbers understate them. Consumer property portals and website builders appear as noise.

**Complaints in the tools developers pay for:**
- **Price jumps after lock-in (owner voice):** Onsite: "the developer suddenly makes the app a paid one… 'either pay 35k a year or we delete your data'"; Powerplay: "too expensive for small construction owners… yearly subscription has become monthly subscription." Price complaints: Onsite 21, Powerplay 10.
- **Missing features (largest theme):** Onsite 38, Powerplay 26 ("add category wise manpower… mark attendance… maintain total till date purchased").
- **Slowness and bugs:** Onsite 22 slow/sync, 14 bugs ("gives timeout error"); Buildesk "lagging for every click… to make a call from this crm you will have to 4 clicks"; Leadrat "crashed 10 times a day minimum, support is 0."
- **CRMs that don't fit real estate:** Buildesk: "Poor understanding of Real Estate business… not willing to make any standard changes."

**Takeaway:** developers pay for site and sales tools (around ₹35K–1.2L a year) and complain about price hikes, slowness and missing features. None of these apps covers RERA compliance or buyer payment schedules well. Buildesk is the only one that touches post-sales, and its app reviews are weak.
