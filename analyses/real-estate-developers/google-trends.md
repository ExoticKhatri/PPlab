## Insights

- **"real estate" +29% YoY** (12-month average 64 vs 50), but the rising queries are investing and share prices, not development. **"home loan" -9%**, a softer demand signal for new housing.
- **"RERA" +1%** (steady, and large for a regulatory term at about half of "builder"). Rising: "rera project search" +500%, "rera search" +250%, "rera approval" +140%. Buyers increasingly check projects on RERA portals, which raises the cost to developers of stale QPR data.
- **"builder" +3%,** but its related searches are polluted by "resume builder", "app builder" and "AI builder", so read it with care.
- "real estate CRM", "construction management software", "channel partner" and "plotting" are too small for Trends. Rising CRM queries are commercial: "best crm software for real estate" +180%, "top crm for real estate" +160%. For construction software: Buildertrend and "contractor foreman" (Breakout).
- **Regions:** Maharashtra leads every developer term (real estate, RERA, builder, CRM), followed by Haryana, Telangana, Karnataka, Gujarat and Chandigarh. MahaRERA holds about 35% of India's registered projects, so Maharashtra is the place to start.
