## Insights

**Coverage:** 108 developers in Indore, Jaipur, Lucknow, Nagpur and Surat (every search hit its 15-place cap). 996 reviews read, 727 with text. Categories: builders & construction company (70), real estate developer (33).

**Digitally mature:** **84% have a website** (the highest of any group analysed), 93% list a phone, median 79 reviews per place, and owners reply to 30% of reviews (60 of 241 low-rated ones). Developers care about reputation because buyers check them.

**What buyers complain about** (241 reviews at ≤2★, mostly from flat and plot buyers, who are the developer's customers): rude / no response 59, **payment / refund disputes 46**, **possession delays 42**, construction quality 38, maintenance 30, fraud claims 24, registry / documents 13.
- Payment disputes: "now they are asking me to pay development fee of 150/- per sqft which is totally baseless"; "We made full payment 12 years ago, but the registry has still not been completed. Despite repeated follow-ups over the years…"
- Promises vs delivery: "the project has not delivered several things that were promised at the time of booking."
- Follow-ups ignored: "inspite of regular follow up his service and behavior…"

**Takeaway:** the developer's weak spot is **post-sales communication**: what is due, why it is due (milestones), what was promised, and where documents stand. That communication failure drives disputes over payments and possession. A system that ties each buyer's demands to verified construction milestones, with updates and documents in one place, would reduce both late payments and bad reviews. That is inferred from buyer complaints, not stated by developers.
