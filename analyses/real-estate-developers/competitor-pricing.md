## Insights

**Published prices (vendors' own pages):**
- **Leadrat:** ₹2,299/user/month for 10 users, falling to ₹1,499 at 100 users, billed yearly (10 users = ₹2,75,880/year). Same features at every tier.
- **StrategicERP:** ₹4,000/user/month, billed yearly (Standard plan); claims 1,000+ developers.
- **Onsite:** ₹12,000/user/year (Business) and ₹15,000/user/year (Professional) plus GST; minimum ₹60,000–75,000/year for 5 users; enterprise from ₹12 lakh/year. Add-ons such as GPS and face attendance cost ₹20,000/year.

**Techjockey listings:** Buildesk ₹1,000/user/month (annual only); Powerplay from ₹71,999/year; Sell.Do listed at ₹2,123; RDash listed at ₹88,500.

**Demo-led (no public price):** Sell.Do, Farvision ("Price Available on Request"), Buildesk, Nway, RDash, Paramantra.

**Price band developers already pay:** sales CRMs cost ₹1,000–2,300 per user per month, so a 10-seat team pays ₹10,000–23,000/month. ERPs cost ~₹4,000 per user per month. Site apps cost ₹60,000–1,20,000 a year. Per-user pricing is the norm. A per-project price (as suggested in the README) would stand out and track the developer's revenue better.

**Reviews on Techjockey** are few (Leadrat 23, Buildesk 3, Onsite 1) and mostly 5★, so they are useful for features, not for complaints. Play Store reviews remain the better complaint source.
