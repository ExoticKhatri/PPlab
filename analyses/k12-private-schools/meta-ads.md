Counts below are approximate (±10–15%): a few schools showing off their own STEM labs get tagged as sellers.

- **The group is on Meta itself:** ~300 schools pay for admission ads (e.g. a Nashik CBSE school's "Admission open · Fee Rs 30,000 (Nursery) · No donation" ad has run 669 days).
- **Language:** 255 ads in Hindi script and 135 in other Indian scripts, so owners in tier-2/3 India are being reached in their own language.
