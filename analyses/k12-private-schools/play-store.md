## Insights

**Clean-up first:** app discovery pulled in 7 irrelevant apps (games like "Scary Teacher 3D", "Teacher Simulator", Google Family Link, study planners). Excluding them leaves **33 school apps and 9,319 complaints** (≤3★). 17,729 of all reviews are from the last 12 months, so this is current.

**Competitor landscape (by downloads):** Google Classroom (100M+), Teachmint (10M+, 4.5★), ParentSquare (5M+, US), then the Indian school ERPs' parent apps at 1M+ each — CampusCare (127k ratings, 4.0★), Neverskip "School Parent App" (4.3★), LEAD (4.6★), NextOS (4.5★), MyClassboard (MCB), Edunext. **Every school ERP app is free to download with no in-app purchases** — the school pays the vendor, parents never do.

| Complaint theme (school apps only) | Complaints | Share |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Crashes, errors, won't open | 1,952 | 20.9% |
| Forced / broken updates | 1,053 | 11.3% |
| Login, password, OTP, school code | 956 | 10.3% |
| Homework & attachments don't load | 783 | 8.4% |
| Slow / loading | 595 | 6.4% |
| Missing notifications | 552 | 5.9% |
| Fee payment (failures, receipts) | 425 | 4.6% |
| Support doesn't respond | 234 | 2.5% |

**Who is speaking:** across all 11,262 complaints only ~136 read like school admins and ~63 like teachers, vs ~583 explicit parents (the rest unclear). Under 2% is the buyer's voice — these reviews show what the school's *customers* suffer, not what the owner thinks.

Real quotes:

- "I submitted school fees through this app. But not received fees receipt even after 4 days. Transaction was successful…" — School Diary, 1★
- "When we opened the app it shows that you are not part of any organization… It's irritating as a teacher when everyone is able to access." — SkoolBeep, 1★
- "There is no option to change the standard of the class. My son was in 2nd std. Now he is in 3rd." — SkoolBeep, 1★

**What breaks in the tools schools pay for:** basic reliability (~50% of complaints are crashes, updates, login, slowness), and the one job parents rely on — paying fees and getting a receipt.
