## Insights

- **School ERPs span a wide range:** from ₹5,000/year for small schools (School ERP INDIA, 0–300 students) to **₹50,000–1,00,000/year + GST at Fedena** (≈₹4,200–8,300/month).
- **The big names (Teachmint, Entab, MyClassboard) and the fee-collection fintech Jodo publish no prices**. Jodo sells on outcomes ("₹150 Cr+ in error-free fee collections" for one academy).
- **Price band:** a fee-collection product at ₹4,000/month (the guide's entry price) sits inside what established schools already pay for an ERP; ₹10,000/month after expansion sits just above Fedena's top plan.
