## Insights

- **Parent/school apps are growing:** "school app" interest roughly doubled year on year (avg 61.8 vs 31.3, +97%), the strongest signal here.
- **School ERP is flat** (+4%) and "school admission" steady (+11%, seasonal). "cbse affiliation" +9%.
- **Niche terms have too little data:** "fee management software" (+1667%) and "online fee payment school" go from ~0 to ~1 — ignore those percentages.
- **Where:** Delhi leads "school erp" and "school admission"; Haryana, Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan, Jharkhand and Uttarakhand follow for ERP. "school app" is strongest in Punjab, Tamil Nadu, Chandigarh, Andhra Pradesh. North India (Delhi NCR, Haryana, UP, Rajasthan) is the first region to target.
- **Related searches are mostly logins** ("erp login vivekanand school", "lexicon school erp login", "ptc school erp") — parents and staff searching for their school's existing ERP portal. That shows a large installed base of ERPs, consistent with the crowded ERP market on Meta.
