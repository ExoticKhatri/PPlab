## Insights

**Landscape:** construction calculators and site apps (Onsite 1M+, Powerplay 1M+, Superlabor attendance 1M+) plus tender-alert apps: **BidAssist** (100K+, 1,804 ratings, owned by OfBusiness), TenderNews (50K+), Tender247 (100K+), TenderKart, TenderShark and others. The government's own **GeM Seller app is rated 2.0★**.

**Complaints, in their words:**
- **GeM Seller (281 complaints in 363 reviews, 133 about login and loading):** "After log in, it's unable to load all elements of home screen. No options are visible for either Bids or Products"; "it always throws network error." Sellers do GeM work on desktop.
- **Paid tender alerts disappoint (BidAssist: 124 complaints, 21 about price):** "Highly misleading. They don't say in the pricing that access to tender results is not included… they don't upload all tenders"; "I subscribed for custom plans for 2 years… After payment no help." TenderNews: "paid subscription nahi lena fraud company hai" (don't take their paid subscription; it's a fraud company).
- **Tender247:** "more costly, less resources"; "Downloading button doesn't work."
- **Labour apps:** Superlabor complaints about autopay mandates ("₹199 mandate… without payment app does not start").

**Takeaway:** contractors pay small amounts (hundreds of rupees a month) for tender alerts and feel cheated by them. The official GeM app barely works. No app tracks what happens *after* a tender is won (EMD, security deposit, bills, payments).
