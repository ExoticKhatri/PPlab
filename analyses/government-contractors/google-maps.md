## Insights

**Coverage:** 122 places in Lucknow, Patna, Bhopal, Jaipur and Raipur. **The search mostly returned house builders**: 84 of 122 are "Real Estate Builders & Construction Company", only 9 are "Contractor", and 6 are government offices. Government works contractors rarely list themselves on Maps or attract public reviews.

**Snapshot:** 64% have a website, median 71 reviews, owners reply to 40%.

**Low reviews (125 at ≤2★)** come from homeowners hiring builders: payment / extra charges 29, quality 26, no response 25, delays 19, fraud 7.

**Takeaway:** Maps does not reach this group. It cannot size it, and the reviews are about private home construction. Group size has to come from registers (CPWD/state PWD enlistment lists, GeM seller counts) instead.
