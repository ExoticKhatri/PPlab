# Exporters (India): group analysis

Collected 2 Nov 2026 across 7 platforms in 29 minutes ([config.json](config.json)). Per-platform detail: [Meta ads](meta-ads.md) · [Play Store](play-store.md) · [YouTube](youtube.md) · [Google search](google-search.md) · [Google Trends](google-trends.md) · [Reddit](reddit.md) · [Google Maps](google-maps.md).

**Data collected:**
- Meta: 1,189 ads from 586 advertisers
- Play Store: 35 apps, 15,421 reviews (only 6 apps are relevant)
- YouTube: 76 videos, 9,015 comments
- Google search: 1,296 autocomplete suggestions, 6 result pages
- Google Trends: 8 terms
- Reddit: 856 posts, 2,566 comments
- Google Maps: 104 exporters, 979 reviews (Tiruppur, Surat, Moradabad, Ludhiana, Rajkot)

## 1. The group

Manufacturer-exporters and merchant exporters shipping goods abroad: garments, textiles, handicrafts, brass, engineering goods, food, and e-commerce (CSB-V courier) sellers. They clear customs through CHAs (customs agents) and get paid through banks or platforms such as Skydo and Payoneer. After each shipment they must:
- close the shipping bill in RBI's EDPMS once payment arrives;
- get the eBRC (proof of payment) or FIRC;
- claim RoDTEP and duty drawback (export incentives worth ~0.3–3.9% of FOB value);
- claim the IGST/ITC refund;
- keep an LUT (the GST bond for exporting without paying tax) filed every year.

Exporters handle much more money per business than the other groups analysed, so they can pay more.

**Size: estimate.** There are 40 lakh+ IEC holders in total (TaxGuru), but the active-exporter count is not published. An estimate of **~1.5 lakh active exporters** is used here; this needs a source (DGFT, EPCs).

**Digital maturity:** 76% of exporters on Maps have a website.

## 2. Can we reach them? Yes, in clusters; on Meta for new exporters only

- **Meta:**
  - About 80 course and coaching sellers target *would-be* exporters.
  - For established exporters, only **Skydo** (payments) advertises steadily.
  - The compliance and refund niche has 4 advertisers: Axodian, Y2kexim, Mygstrefund, Export Import Leadership Institute. It is open, but unproven.
- **Clusters:**
  - Exporters sit in known hubs: Tiruppur, Surat, Moradabad, Rajkot, Ludhiana, plus Gujarat, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu and Delhi, which lead every Trends term.
  - Export promotion councils (EPCs), CHAs, CAs and AD banks are natural channels.
- **Search:** heavy, specific status searches such as "rodtep not received" and "edpms shipping bill status" make SEO and content a cheap channel.

**What this means:** exporters already pay for many fragments:
- payment fees ($19–29 per payment);
- bank closure charges (₹200–1,000 per bill);
- refund consultants (success fee);
- buyer data ($250–1,500);
- CAs and CHAs (not measured).

Documentation software is cheap (~₹1,000–5,000). **Nobody sells one product that tracks every shipment from shipping bill to payment, eBRC, EDPMS closure, RoDTEP, drawback and IGST refund.** The one tracking tool seen (Axodian) is demo-only and barely advertised.

## 5. Opportunities spotted in the data

Customers needed are for **₹50 Cr/year** (₹50 Cr ÷ (price × 12)), as a share of the ~1.5 lakh estimated active exporters.

1. **Export money-and-compliance desk** (degree 2, linked to both cash and compliance). Every shipping bill gets one row, from shipment to cash:
   - payment received and matched (FIRC/eBRC);
   - EDPMS closed;
   - RoDTEP and drawback credited;
   - IGST/ITC refund status;
   - alerts before realisation deadlines and the yearly LUT;
   - bank-ready closure packs, so closure stops costing ₹200–1,000 per bill;
   - a CA/CHA login.

   Evidence: pains #1, #2 and #3 on Reddit, YouTube, Google and Meta, with one barely-advertised competitor.
   - **Suggested price: ₹8,000/month to start; ₹20,000/month after expansion** (+ export documents, buyer-payment tracking with factoring partners, multiple entities and AD banks). Volume tiers: ~₹4,000 for ≤20 shipping bills/month, ₹8,000 for ≤100, custom above that.
     - **Customers needed:** 5,209 (3.5%) at the entry price; 2,084 (1.4%) after expansion.
     - **Why this price:** an exporter closing 50 bills/month at ₹400 per bill pays ₹20,000/month in bank closure fees alone (Reddit's ₹400 figure; volumes are an estimate). One missed LUT cost ₹15.77 lakh, and unclaimed RoDTEP is 0.3–3.9% of FOB. It costs more than documentation tools (₹1,000–5,000) because it recovers and protects money; the documentation tools only produce paperwork.
   - **Risks:**
     - **No official APIs** for ICEGATE, DGFT and EDPMS (bank-side). Data may need uploads, bank statements or browser automation; this must be validated first.
     - Banks and CAs may resist the change.
     - RoDTEP rates are being cut (Trends -15%), which makes that part of the claim smaller.
     - The group size is unsourced.
2. **Export documentation generator** (invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, all from one shipment record).
   - **Suggested price: ₹2,000/month to start; ₹5,000/month after expansion.**
     - **Customers needed:** 20,834 (13.9%) at the entry price; 8,334 (5.6%) after expansion.
     - **Why this price:** it sits beside Expodite and Focus (~₹1,000–5,000) and free Excel templates.
   - Fails the target math alone. It is better as a module of opportunity 1.
3. **Export receivables and buyer-payment protection** (track what each buyer owes, chase overdue payments, refer to factoring partners).
   - **Suggested price: ₹5,000/month plus a referral share on financing.**
     - **Customers needed:** 8,334 (5.6%), before any referral revenue.
   - Risk: lenders (Credlix, M1 NXT) and payment firms (Skydo) are moving in.

Opportunity 1 is the only one that clears the target math with a realistic share. Opportunities 2 and 3 are the expansion modules that take it from ₹8,000 to ₹20,000.

## 6. Data gaps

- **Group size:** the active-exporter count is unsourced (40 lakh+ IEC holders in total). It should be checked with DGFT/EPC data.
- **Data access:** ICEGATE, DGFT and EDPMS integration options were not researched. This is the main technical risk.
- **Play Store:** mostly trading and utility apps; exporters do this work on desktop portals.
- **Maps:** reviewers are domestic buyers and job seekers, not the exporters' overseas clients.
- **Reddit:** exporter subreddits are small (largest 5.5K members). The strongest posts are from e-commerce (CSB-V) exporters, whose bill volumes are extreme.
- **Not collected:** EPC member forums, LinkedIn, CA/CHA communities, bank trade-desk pricing.
