## Insights

- **Payment platforms charge per transaction:** PayGlocal from 2.75% (cards) or $10 per multi-currency payment; Razorpay from 2%; Skydo $19–29 per payment (published, from the earlier check). An exporter receiving 20 payments a month pays roughly $400–600/month in fees.
- **Trade data:** Volza from $1,500/year (Techjockey).
- **Documentation and compliance tools hide their prices:** Axodian, Shipzy and Export Genius publish none; Expodite lists ₹1,180.
- **Price band:** exporters already pay per transaction and per shipment (bank closure ₹200–1,000 per bill), not per seat. A per-shipping-bill or tiered-volume price fits how they already pay.
