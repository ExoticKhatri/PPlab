## Insights

- **"export business" +37% YoY** (12-month average 62 vs 45). Interest in starting is rising; Gujarat leads, then Puducherry, Tamil Nadu and Maharashtra.
- **"export documentation" +265%** (25 vs 7), the fastest-growing term. Its related searches are mostly jobs and procedure PDFs, so part of this is career interest, not only exporters.
- **"RoDTEP" -15% and "duty drawback" -33%.** Interest is falling, consistent with reports of RoDTEP rate cuts; "duty drawback status" is a top related search.
- **"IEC code" +3%** (steady flow of new registrations; Delhi leads).
- **"tariff" -28%,** but rising searches are about US tariffs (US Supreme Court tariff decision: Breakout). US tariffs are a live worry for exporters.
- EDPMS and export finance are too small to measure in Trends, although Google autocomplete shows 216 EDPMS/BRC suggestions. These are niche, high-intent terms.
- Regions: Gujarat, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Delhi and Dadra & Nagar Haveli lead every exporter term. That is where outreach should start.
