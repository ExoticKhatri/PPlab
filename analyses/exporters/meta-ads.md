**Can Meta reach exporters?** Yes for *new* exporters: courses and IEC registration dominate, in English (77%) plus Hindi, Marathi, Malayalam, Tamil and Gujarati. For *established* exporters, few companies keep paying to reach them. Only Skydo runs a sustained campaign, and the compliance and refund niche has 4 advertisers. That makes it **cheap to test but unproven**.

**Pains sellers lead with:**
- Money stuck: "Stop waiting 60-90 days for export payments"; "Export Order Ready Hai. Payment Abhi Tak Nahi Aayi?" (the export order is ready, but the payment still hasn't arrived).
- Refund stuck: "Refund stuck kyun hai? Shipping Bill mismatch, EGM, PFMS…" (why is your refund stuck?).
- FX losses: "hidden forex charges".
- Tracking is done manually: "track shipping bills, eBRCs, and EDPMS manually using Excel sheets."

**Prices seen in ads:** IndiaMART buy-lead software ₹5,000/month; export courses ₹199–₹20,000; Razorpay international payments ~3%.
