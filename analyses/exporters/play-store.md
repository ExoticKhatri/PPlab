## Insights

**Mostly noise, which is itself a finding.** "Export" and "trade" searches return trading apps (Olymptrade, Zerodha, eToro) and file-exporter utilities. Only 6 of 35 apps serve exporters, and they are small:

- **ExportersIndia (B2B directory):** 500K+ downloads. **374 complaints in 1,000 reviews**, almost all about paid memberships that brought no buyers: "i purchased it of 3 year plan and total loss… No lead no calls"; "have paid a substantial amount… now I am being contacted for renewal by these shameless people."
- **Import Export Solutions** (50K+): registration does not work ("I try to register for 4 days but it can't"); "there should be a simpler version… duty calculator."
- **HDFC Trade Finance on Mobile** (100K+): login errors ("Doesn't work at all. Keeps giving error").
- **Indiaport, Ninja Global Import Export:** small; OTP failures, crashes, slow profile upgrades.

**Takeaway:** exporters do their compliance and finance work on desktop portals (ICEGATE, DGFT, bank trade portals), through CAs and customs agents (CHAs), and in Excel, not in mobile apps. The only paid apps they talk about are **buyer-lead memberships, which disappoint**. There is no app competitor for compliance tracking, and Play Store will not be the main channel.
