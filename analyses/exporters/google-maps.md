## Insights

**Coverage:** 104 exporters in Tiruppur, Surat, Moradabad, Ludhiana and Rajkot (every search hit its 15-place cap, so more exist). 979 reviews read, 560 with text. Categories: import export company (30), exporter (17), garment exporter (12), handicraft exporter (9), manufacturers.

**More digital than distributors:** **76% have a website** (vs 39% for distributors), 89% list a phone, median 36 reviews, and owners reply to 24% of reviews.

**What reviewers complain about** (117 reviews at ≤2★): rude behaviour / no response 26, payment/refund 25, delivery 17, jobs/salary 17, quality 16, fraud 9. Almost all reviewers are **domestic wholesale buyers and job seekers** (for example a "₹20,000 return parcel" at a Surat clothing wholesaler); overseas buyers don't review on Maps.

**Takeaway:** Maps confirms exporters cluster densely in known hubs and are reachable by phone and web. It says nothing about their export compliance pains, which show up on Reddit, YouTube and Google. For outreach, the cluster lists (Tiruppur garments, Moradabad brass, Surat textiles, Rajkot engineering, Ludhiana hosiery) are useful.
