## Insights

**Verdict: Meta is full of brands looking for distributors, and almost empty of tools sold to distributors.** 996 ads from 622 advertisers. Most are brands **recruiting distributors and super stockists** (EVs, water, snacks, beverages, tea, pipes, electricals, solar: "Become a Super Stockist", "1000+ Distributors Network", "up to 30% more margins") and wholesale supply platforms (Amazon Business 12 ads, 317 days; METRO Wholesale; Flipkart Wholesale Best Price: "7 days FREE credit"; B2B accessory apps).
