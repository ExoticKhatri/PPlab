## Insights

**Coverage:** 138 distributors and wholesalers in Indore, Jaipur, Lucknow, Ahmedabad and Nagpur (2 searches × 5 cities, 15 each; every search hit its cap). Collected in 25 minutes with 3 tabs. 1,009 reviews read, 688 with text. Categories: FMCG goods wholesaler (47), wholesaler (14), food products supplier (8), FMCG manufacturer (7), plus electrical, plastic and spice wholesalers.

**The least digital group analysed so far:** only **39% have a website** (vs 71–98% for hospitals, factories and dealerships), the median is only **26 reviews** per place, and owners reply to 35%. 92% list a phone. Distributors are reached through phone, WhatsApp, salesmen and brands, not search.

**What reviewers complain about** (128 reviews at ≤2★; most reviewers are retailers or walk-in bulk buyers): rude behaviour / no response 34, price/MRP 22, payment/advance/fraud 15, delivery and supply 13, near-expiry or poor quality 13, employment 10.
- Near-expiry stock: "You will get only products which are near to expiry… Grocery has the worst situation here only 3-4 months of ex[piry]."
- Advances not honoured: "I paid ₹50,000 in advance… for FMCG stock. It has been 5 months… No stock received, No refund given, No proper response."
- Returns and exchanges refused: "if you ask them to replace the utensil the owner doesn't speak to you"; "Exchanging item is a headache."

**Takeaway:** Maps confirms the group is large and present in every city, but it says little about the distributor's own pains (collections, credit), because reviewers are their customers. It does show **low digital maturity**: a tool for this group must work over WhatsApp and phone, not depend on a web presence.
