## Insights

**Collector note:** the first run ranked apps by review count and kept only consumer billing apps (Khatabook, Vyapar, myBillBook). The collector now ranks by **relevance to the search terms first** (`playStore.rankBy`, default "relevance"). The re-run returned the real B2B tools: 35 apps, ~6,000 reviews from order-booking, sales-force and collection apps, 2,127 complaints.

**Who the apps serve**
- **Brands' field sales teams (sales-force automation):** FieldAssist General Trade (100K+, 4.1★), FieldAssist SFA, FA Flo, FA Analytics, **Bizom One (Distiman)** (100K+, **3.5★**), FieldSense (100K+, 3.9★, 10,878 ratings), SalesOn, Field Force Connect, many small field-tracking apps.
- **Distributor and wholesaler tools (order taking and billing):** Marg's **eOrder – Salesman Order App** (100K+, 4.3★) and **eRetail – B2B Order & Payment** (100K+), Distributo (4.7★, small), "Distributor App", "DMS", Order Book, Sales Order Booking App (2.5★), EasySol, MyKiranaBuddy B2B.
- **Collections:** "Collection App" (10K+, 4.2★), CF Collections (a lender's app, 2.9★).

**What breaks** (2,127 complaints; keyword themes, overlapping): login/crash/bugs 324, **slow/sync/offline 229**, GPS/location/battery 190, orders/schemes/prices 149, payments/ledger 46.

**Takeaway:** order taking on the street is digitised by brand-mandated SFA apps that salesmen dislike (slow, offline failures, GPS). Distributor-owned tools are Marg-style billing and order apps. **Collections have no strong product**: generic collection apps are small or lender-owned.
