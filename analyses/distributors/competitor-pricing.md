## Insights

- **Billing and ordering tools cost ₹290–1,150/month:** Vyapar ₹367–400/month, myBillBook ₹291–333/month (Pro Max from ₹570), Busy ₹5,000–12,000 a year for most businesses. Marg is a one-time licence of ₹5,550–13,900 plus ₹3,000 per extra user.
- **Collections:** Credflow's Tally plan is ₹4,999/year (≈₹420/month) on Techjockey.
- **Field-sales apps (Bizom, FieldAssist) are demo-led** and sold to brands. No public price.
- **Price band confirmed:** distributors already pay ₹300–1,000/month for software. That supports the guide's decision to deprioritise this group: a ₹3,000–8,000 product would be a 3–10× jump.
