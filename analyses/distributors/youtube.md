- **Cash flow when selling to retailers:** a manufacturer distributing directly: "problem is in cash flow management, as business is small, major sa[les on credit]…" (👍42); advice: "provide channel financing… distributor ko credit mil jaega" (👍3).
- **Thin margins and slow payment from brands:** "ITC 2 se 3% profit hai aur enka payment 3 mahine me milta hai" (👍2); pharma sub-stockist margins "6 to 8%… have to give at least 4% to chemist" (👍24).
- **Staff theft:** "worker dhoka de gaye… payment ko galle me na rakh kar apane paas rakh liye" (workers pocket the collected cash; 👍4).

**Takeaway:** distributors live on **thin margins (2–8%) and long credit**. The money is stuck in the market with retailers, and collection by salesmen leaks. Quick commerce and B2B platforms come up as the new competition.
