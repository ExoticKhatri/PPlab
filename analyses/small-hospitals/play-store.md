## Insights

**Main finding: hospital management software for small Indian hospitals is not a Play Store category.** Two runs with hospital-specific searches (HMS, HMIS, nursing home software, hospital EMR, IPD management) still kept mostly consumer apps (Where is my Train, Samsung Health, NoiseFit, XCIPTV, Netmeds). That happened because the collector keeps the most-reviewed apps, and real hospital tools have only a few hundred ratings. The vendors advertising on Meta (Healthray, Shivam Medisoft, Myopd, TatvaCare, etc.) sell desktop/web software, so their apps either don't exist or have too few reviews to appear.

**What breaks**
- **Doctors' EMR at the point of care:** "Application hang in last step of saving prescription many times, not able to message or print prescription at last which is very embarrassing in front of patients" (HealthPlix, 👍135); "They take money from patients but do not credit in your account. Costumer support is so poor" (HealthPlix, 👍69).
- **Government HMIS login:** "the app doesn't auto detect OTP, dosent even take you to login page after OTP page… the login button has no functionality" (NIMS HMIS, 👍69); "There's no tab to report locally purchased and issued medicines… not updated in the HMIS" (Railway-HMIS, 👍62).
- **Patient-facing hospital apps don't match operations:** "appointment timings are arbitrary, and not what the doctors actually follow" (Max MyHealth, 👍164); "There is no instant pathology reports upload for ipd… One needs to run to reception every time to get reports" (Max MyHealth, 👍62).
- **Government scheme apps have the worst ratings:** the Ayushman App gets 54% complaints and Ayushman CAPF 75%. Patients struggle to get and use their cards, which then becomes the hospital desk's problem.

**Takeaway:** the hospital software market here is desktop/web and sold through demos and calls, not app stores. Play shows that what exists is clunky at the point of care, and that patients reach hospitals through government health-ID and scheme apps (ABHA, Ayushman) that are growing fast but work poorly. Collector note: for B2B groups like this, Play discovery should rank by relevance or use explicit `appIds` instead of the most-reviewed apps (logged as a TODO).
