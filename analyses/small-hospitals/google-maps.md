## Insights

**Coverage:** 199 places across 10 searches (2 queries × 5 tier-2 cities, 20 each). Every search "hit the limit", so many more exist. 1,944 reviews were read (10 lowest-rated per place), 1,753 with text. **Mixed sample, read with care:**
- **"nursing home" (100 places):** 33 nursing homes and about 30 hospitals, but also **29 home-care/nurse agencies** (Indian Maps mixes in home nursing). Their median review count is low, and only 48% have a website.
- **"multispeciality hospital" (100 places):** 80 independent or small-chain hospitals (74 with a website) and **19 big corporate hospitals** (Narayana, Max, Medanta, Paras, Apollo Medics, Jupiter, Shalby, Regency…), which hold most of the reviews (2.9 lakh of 5 lakh). I report corporate hospitals separately below.

**Digital maturity:** independent hospitals have websites (93% of the multispeciality sample, against 48% for the nursing-home sample), phones are listed for 94%, and owners reply to **37%** of reviews at non-corporate places. Ratings average 4.62 with a median of 331 reviews per place. Hospitals actively work their Google reviews.

**Takeaway:** for small and mid-size independent hospitals, patients' complaints after staff behaviour are about **process: OPD waiting, billing transparency, cashless/scheme handling and discharge delays.** The discharge–TPA–mediclaim step is where hospital operations and insurance paperwork visibly fail the patient. Pure nursing homes get fewer process complaints (they're smaller and simpler).
