## Insights

- **Hospital software rarely publishes prices.** Only NiftyHMS (₹1,500/user/month on Techjockey) shows a software price.
- **Implementation is expensive:** Healthray publishes service rates (training ₹15,000/day, a dedicated consultant ₹2 lakh/month, customisation packages ₹5.25–34.5 lakh). Hospitals that buy an HMS also pay heavily to set it up.
- **Claims and RCM players (Nimblefin and others) publish nothing.** Their pricing is likely a share of claims recovered (inferred, not confirmed).
- **Price band:** ₹1,500 per user per month for basic HMS, plus lakhs in services. A ₹8,000/month claims desk is small next to implementation costs.
