## Insights

**Caveat:** "nursing home" means elderly care in the US. The biggest comment pile (706) is on "The Deadly Monetization of Nursing Homes" (How Money Works), which is irrelevant here. I excluded it and 4 other off-topic videos (senior care, home-nursing jobs, a generic healthcare course). The rest is **66 videos and 2,471 comments**, a mix of hospital owners/staff, doctors and patients.

**What draws views**
- **Hospital economics:** "How Hospitals Make Money: KIMS, Costs & Hospital Business Truth" (Raj Shamani, 3.4 lakh) and "Why Do Hospitals Charge So Much? Hospital Owner Explains the Truth" (56K).
- **How to start a hospital or nursing home** (Hindi): several videos of 1–1.5 lakh views on licences, costs and "can a non-doctor open a hospital".
- **Software demos get views but no discussion:** Adroit eHospital (2.75 lakh views, 7 comments), Logicpro "How Hospital Management System works?" (1.1 lakh, 15), NiftyHMS (8.7K, 4), AroBuddy. Same pattern as CA firms: people watch demos but don't talk about them.

- **Insurance claim rejection** (patient side): Gurleen Kaur Tikku, TPA Insights (16K–60K views).

- **Patients come through middlemen:** "हॉस्पिटल खोलने में कोई दिक्कत नहीं लेकिन बिना दलाल के सहयोग के मरीज नहीं आ पाते" (opening a hospital is easy, but patients don't come without a broker; 👍20).

- Software vocabulary is confused: "That is not a hospital management system. That is a clinical management system. Hospital management system is broader… to sustain the business of a hospital" (👍16).
- Patients distrust insurers and hospitals: "Companies premium le leti hai or baad me claim reject kar deti hain" (👍19); "check the blacklisted hospital list first" when you have a heart attack (👍8).

**Takeaway:** for small hospitals, the operational pain that shows up most is **insurance/government-scheme paperwork**: Ayushman/PMJAY portals, TPA pre-auth and claims. Software is watched but not discussed, and patient acquisition runs through referrals and middlemen.
