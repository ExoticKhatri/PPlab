## Insights

- **Government health schemes are the growth story:** "ayushman bharat" **+114% YoY** (peak 100) and "abha" (the ABDM health ID) **+41%**. Rising related searches are all ABHA/Ayushman card terms (Breakout). Patients are getting cards in large numbers, so more patients arrive at hospitals with scheme coverage. Hospitals then need empanelment, ABDM-compliant software and scheme claims handling.
- **"nabh" +10%** and "nursing home" +8%: steady. Rising NABH searches point to new standards and cheaper entry routes: "nabh 6th edition pdf" +550%, "nabh ayush entry level certification" +2,350%.
- **"tpa" -13%:** still a sizeable term (average 10.5). Rising queries are new TPAs (Volo Health Insurance TPA: Breakout; Ewa TPA +1,450%) and "medi assist tpa preauth fo[rm]". The TPA landscape is shifting, adding more portals for hospitals to deal with.
- **Software terms are too small to measure** ("hospital management system", "hospital software", "emr software": too little data). Related searches are dominated by student projects ("hospital management system project / er diagram / github"), not buyers.
- **Regions:** hospital software interest leads in the South and West (Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Kerala, Karnataka, Maharashtra). Ayushman interest leads in West Bengal and the North-East. TPA interest leads in Haryana, Delhi and Gujarat. "nursing home" as a term is strongest in West Bengal, where it is the everyday word for a small private hospital.
