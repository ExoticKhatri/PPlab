## Insights

**Landscape:** the installed base of college ERPs shows up as student apps.
- **MasterSoft:** Student Diary Cloud (1M+), MasterSoft Cloud (100K+) and many white-label apps for individual universities (YCCE, SVNIT, HITS, SGTU, PUB).
- **Camu:** MyCamu Lite (500K+, 12,194 ratings), MyCamu, Camu Staff.
- **Others:** Edumarshal (100K+), Campus 365, CampusMan, plus university-built ERPs (Sathyabama, GNIOT, GUNI).
- Ratings are low across the board: 2.5–3.8★.

**Complaints (2,322 at ≤3★ across 21 ERP apps; almost all from students):** login, slowness, crashes and server errors 452; notifications and sync 211; attendance 130; fees and payments 61; results and marks 48. Only 20 mention faculty or staff.
- "HITS ERP: Not working, full glitiches… taking lakhs of money for fees but not even maintaining the single app."
- "YCCE Student Diary: …click on forgot password… you are logged out for the rest of your life."
- "Campus 365: Not a deployment ready application but still they did it anyway."

**Takeaway:** the ERP market is consolidated (MasterSoft and Camu hold the visible base) and the products are weak where students touch them. The buyer, the college, isn't the one reviewing, so these complaints say more about switching opportunity than about what management pays for.
