## Insights

- **Compliance interest is falling:** "NAAC" -47%, "NIRF" -30%, "AISHE" -39% (12-month average vs the year before). The fall in NAAC searches matches the 2025 move from letter grades to binary accreditation with maturity levels, which may reduce the yearly scramble around grades (inferred).
- **"APAAR ID" -25%,** with Breakout searches for "apaar id download" and "digilocker apaar id". This is a student ID push driven by government; colleges must register students.
- "college ERP", "NBA accreditation", "admission CRM" and "IQAC" are too small for Trends. Commercial search for college software is low-volume.
- **Takeaway:** no growth tailwind. Accreditation work is steady and mandatory, but public interest is shrinking.
