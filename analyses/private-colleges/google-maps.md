## Insights

**Coverage:** 125 colleges in Indore, Jaipur, Lucknow, Nagpur and Coimbatore (every search hit its cap). 1,214 reviews read, sorted lowest first. Categories: college (54), university (18), engineering school (17), private university (8).

**Highly visible, poorly managed reputations:** **95% have a website** and the **median college has 863 reviews**, far above every other group. Yet owners reply to only **10%** of the reviews read (107 of 1,051 low-rated). Students and parents judge colleges publicly, and colleges mostly don't respond.

**What students and parents complain about** (1,051 reviews at ≤2★; overlapping themes): fees, refunds and documents 316, faculty and teaching 270, management and administration 255, infrastructure 173, placements 106, exams and results 101, false promises 50.
- Refundable deposits not refunded: "security fee of rs 7500 is refundable. But it's been two years…"; "₹10,000 hostel security fee is refundable, but it's a trap."
- Fee changes: "if your fee is X in 1st year then again X+Y in 2nd year"; "They impose penalties without any valid reason."
- Cancellation refunds: "completed all required cancellation formalities. However, my fees have still not been refunded."

**Takeaway:** colleges' public reputation is large, damaged by fee disputes and administration, and barely managed (a 10% reply rate). Reputation feeds admissions, which is the college's revenue. A clear fee ledger per student, with refunds tracked and every review answered, would protect admissions. That link is inferred from student reviews, not stated by colleges.
