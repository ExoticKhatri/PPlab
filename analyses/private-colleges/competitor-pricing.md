## Insights

**Every college ERP is demo-led:** Camu, MasterSoft, Linways, Edumarshal, Meritto, Creatrix Campus and Academia ERP publish no prices. Techjockey shows "price on request" for MasterSoft, Edumarshal, Creatrix and Academia.

**Published prices:**
- **Fedena:** Standard ₹50,000, Premium ₹75,000, Ultimate ₹1,00,000 per year + GST (≈₹4,200–8,300/month). Fedena is aimed at schools and smaller colleges.
- **LeadSquared (widely used for university admissions):** Sales Pro ₹2,500/user/month; Sales Super ₹5,000/user/month. An admissions team of 15 counsellors would pay ₹37,500–75,000/month.

**Price band:** colleges pay enterprise-style, quote-based prices for ERPs (not public; likely lakhs per year for mid-size colleges, unconfirmed) and per-seat prices for admissions CRMs (₹2,500–5,000 per counsellor per month). Of every group so far, this shows the highest per-customer spend, but most of it is hidden behind demos.
