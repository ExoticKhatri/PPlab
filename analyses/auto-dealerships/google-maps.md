## Insights

**Coverage:** 172 places, 1,100 reviews; car showrooms, two-wheeler showrooms and used-car dealers in Pune, Jaipur, Lucknow, Indore and Coimbatore (collected in 27 minutes with 3 tabs). Every search hit its cap. **Dealerships get huge review volumes** (median 1,860 per place) and are digitally active: 81% have websites (98% of car showrooms, 78% of two-wheeler, 63% of used-car dealers), 99% list a phone, and owners reply to **55%** of reviews. Most places are OEM brands (Maruti 18, Tata 10, Suzuki, Yamaha, Honda, Mahindra), plus Cars24 and Spinny hubs.

**Takeaway:** **after-sales service is the dealership's biggest customer-facing failure**, especially for two-wheelers (68% of complaints). Service is also the dealer's most profitable, recurring business. The pattern: poor status updates, slow work, no follow-up, unclear bills. Owners reply to more than half of their reviews, so they care about this.
