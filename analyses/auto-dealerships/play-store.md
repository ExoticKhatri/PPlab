## Insights

**Caveat:** discovery again ranked by review count, so consumer apps fill the list (Moneyview, Ola, OLX, TeamViewer, Google Tasks). The relevant apps fall into three sets.

**Marketplaces (the dealer's competition and channel):** Cars24 (10M+, 4.4★), CarDekho (10M+), CarWale (10M+), Spinny (10M+), BikeDekho, BikeWale, Droom (3.7★), Gaadi Bazaar (Chola), OLX.

- **Digital Showroom (DotPe)** (10M+, 3.9★): small businesses including dealers buying websites/catalogues; complaints about hidden pricing and domain lock-in.

**Workshop / CRM tools:** ARI auto repair (3.8★, invoicing bugs), Repairing Shop Manager, HelloLeads CRM, Zoho CRM, SuperManage (staff attendance).

**Takeaway:** **used-car dealers source stock through auction platforms they don't trust** (inspection misses, hidden charges, opaque bidding, missing registration details). Platform dealer apps are rated 3.3–3.9★. That is a real, money-linked pain for independent dealers, and it sits with the platforms rather than with a software vendor.
