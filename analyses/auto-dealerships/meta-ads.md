## Insights

**Verdict: almost nobody sells software to dealerships on Meta.** 974 ads from 400 advertisers, but the big spenders sell *to car buyers*: Spinny (112 + 16 ads), Maruti Suzuki Arena (18), Tata Motors (15), Tata.ev (9), Truebil (9), Hoora car care (97, selling franchises). **Platforms position against dealers.** Cars24: "Blind trust mat karo dealer pe. Verify karna smart hai" (don't blindly trust the dealer); "Before you accept that dealer's offer, explore your options"; a used-car ad: "Dealer ke chakkar mein time waste mat karo!" Independent used-car dealers compete with well-funded platforms for both buyers and stock.

**Language:** 86% English, 11% Hindi/Hinglish.

**Takeaway:** dealers are not targeted by software vendors on Meta. Authorised dealers run on OEM-mandated systems (sold top-down), and the only active pitches are lead follow-up (Dave.AI), garage management and EV dealership recruitment. The gap that ads show: **lead follow-up and service operations**.
